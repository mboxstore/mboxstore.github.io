Why MBOX Store

Verified before install

Every APK is checked against its publisher's signing certificate and SHA-256 hash before the installer opens. If a file doesn't match, it doesn't install.

Official sources

Apps come from their official publishers — no mods, no repacks, no cracked versions.

Works offline

The catalog is saved on your phone, so you can browse apps even without a connection.

MBOX Pay built in

Support developers and buy premium apps with Telegram Stars or USDT, right inside the store.

How to install

  1. Tap Download above and save the APK file.
  2. Open the file and tap Install. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser — allow it for this install.
  3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway — MBOX Store is distributed outside Google Play, so the warning is expected.
